What Do These Numbers Mean?

📡 Ping (Latency)

Response time of your network. Lower is better for gaming and video calls.

< 20ms — Excellent (Gaming)
< 50ms — Good (Streaming)
> 100ms — Slow (Noticeable Lag)

💡 जितना कम ms, उतना तेज़ response

⬇️ Download Speed

Speed at which data comes to your device. Affects streaming, browsing, and downloads.

> 100 Mbps — Excellent (4K HDR)
> 25 Mbps — Good (HD Streaming)
< 5 Mbps — Slow (Buffering)

💡 जितना ज़्यादा Mbps, उतना अच्छा

⬆️ Upload Speed

Speed at which data goes from your device to internet. Affects video calls and uploads.

> 20 Mbps — Excellent (Live Stream)
> 5 Mbps — Good (Video Calls)
< 1 Mbps — Slow (Uploads Lag)

💡 जितना ज़्यादा Mbps, उतना बेहतर
